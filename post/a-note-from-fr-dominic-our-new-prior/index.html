A Note from Fr. Dominic, Our New Prior

Friday, August 14, 2026

Fr. Dominic sent this letter to the community as he settles into his new role as Prior:

Dear Confreres,

Thank you for your prayers as I take on my new role of service as Prior. Due to other duties in the school, Fr. Bede has graciously agreed to take responsibility for most of the scheduling (I will still handle pastoral coordination, but he will print the schedules). Ordinarily, I will have the morning office hours and Fr. Bede will have the afternoon office hours. Please plan to bring any extraordinary requests or permissions in the morning rather than the afternoon. I will not be present at Sext during the days of school.

A few reminders for all of us:

  1. Travel Forms for overnight trips other than pastoral assignments are signed by the Abbot. Copies must be given to the Prior, the Subprior, the Abbey Business Office, the MC, and the Choirmaster (if you are a chanter).
  2. We owe it to each other to prepare for our duties as Matins Acolyte, Table Reader, and Celebrant. If we cannot be present, we should arrange for or inform a substitute so they can prepare.
  3. The Table Reader should be ready to begin reading after the table blessing. Ordinarily, this duty will require the monk to leave Vespers at the beginning of the Angelus.
  4. Table readers continue reading until the bell — please don't pause for lengthy periods near 6:30.
  5. If you are absent from Matins/Lauds or Vespers, please let me know beforehand if possible.
  6. Cars are ordinarily parked under the carport, not at the back door, except for loading and unloading — and please turn off all accessories when you park.

Pax,
Fr. Dominic, Prior
