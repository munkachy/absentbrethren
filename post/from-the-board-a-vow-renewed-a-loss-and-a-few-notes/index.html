From the Board: A Vow Renewed, a Loss, and a Few Notes

Sunday, August 16, 2026

Brother Joseph Goode renews his vows

Abbot Christian of the Monastery of Christ in the Desert wrote to Abbot Brendan this week: on the evening of August 14, before Compline, Br. Joseph Goode renewed his simple vows for another year, as planned. He'd made a solitude retreat there in the days leading up to it. Abbot Christian wrote that the community is glad to have him, and that he's faithful to the common life and generous in all that's asked of him — "Deo gratias." Worth a prayer of thanksgiving.

Please pray for the repose of Richard Piacin

Richard Piacin passed away recently. He was Fr. Patrick's brother-in-law, married to Fr. Patrick's sister for over fifty years. The funeral will be out of state on September 13.

And a small practical note

The wall thermostats in the monastery cells now display the room's actual temperature — pressing any button switches the display to show the temperature it's set to, rather than the room's current reading.

A new 2026–2027 faculty and staff list for the Prep is also up on the board, for anyone who wants to see who's where this year.
