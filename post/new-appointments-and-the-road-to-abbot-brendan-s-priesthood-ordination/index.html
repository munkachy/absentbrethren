New Appointments, and the Road to Abbot Brendan's Priesthood Ordination

Saturday, August 8, 2026

Two pieces of news from the board this week — new appointments taking effect this weekend, and the full calendar for Abbot Brendan's path to priesthood ordination.

Appointments — Effective Saturday Evening, August 8 (First Vespers of Sunday)

  • Prior: Fr. Dominic
  • Subprior: Fr. Bede
  • Novice Master: Fr. Joel
  • Junior Master: Fr. Joel
  • Procurator: Br. Charles
  • Infirmary Director: Fr. Bede
  • MC: Fr. Paschal

Dates for Abbot Brendan's Priesthood Formation & Abbatial Blessing

  • Institution to the Ministry of Lector — Tuesday, September 1, at 5 p.m. Conventual Mass
  • Institution to the Ministry of Acolyte — Sunday, September 20, at 10:30 a.m. Conventual Mass
  • Diaconate Ordination Retreat at St. Meinrad — Week of October 4
  • Diaconate Ordination — Tuesday, October 20, at 4 p.m., followed by a festive dinner
  • Abbatial Blessing — Saturday, November 21, at 10 a.m.
  • Priesthood Ordination / Mass of Thanksgiving — Saturday, May 29, and Sunday, May 30, 2027

A full year ahead for Abbot Brendan — worth keeping all of it in prayer.
