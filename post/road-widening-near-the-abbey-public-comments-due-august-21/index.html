Road Widening Near the Abbey — Public Comments Due August 21

Friday, August 7, 2026

The Alabama Department of Transportation is moving ahead with a project to widen AL-69 and replace the bridge over Eight Mile Creek near the Abbey, and the community is being asked to submit public comments before an August 21 deadline.

Fr. Paschal and Fr. Bede attended a public meeting on the broader Hwy-278 expansion on August 5 at East Elementary School, alongside area parents and faculty. A few notes from that meeting: right-of-way acquisition is expected to begin in 2027, with construction starting in 2029; the roughly $34 million project runs on federal grants and a congressional earmark, and likely can't grow beyond that; and because of funding limits, the expansion — a five-lane road with curb and gutter — will only run from Fourth Street to about Richard Avenue before tapering back into the existing road, which raises its own concern about simply shifting the traffic bottleneck. Alabama's highway director is retiring, and a meeting with State Senator Garland was discussed as a way to help secure further funding.

Closer to home, the Abbey is asking anyone willing to submit a comment specifically on the AL-69 widening, since it affects the Abbey's own entrance — there's real concern about traffic congestion and about the safety of students, pilgrims, and retreat and festival guests coming in and out, and a push for a traffic light there. Comments can be emailed to AL69-Widening@dot.state.al.us and are due by August 21, 2026.

Worth keeping in prayer — for a safe, sensible resolution at our own front gate.
