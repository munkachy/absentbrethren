Cullman Tourism Filming, and a Road Closure — Notes for September

Thursday, September 10, 2026

Two notes from Fr. Dominic this week:

Cullman tourism will be filming the Office of Sext on Wednesday, September 16 — the community's been asked to make a special effort to be present that day.

And on Saturday, September 19, the Quad Road will be closed all day, morning to night, while it gets striped for parallel parking spots and marked "no parking" zones — meant to keep the road fully accessible even during heavy traffic.
