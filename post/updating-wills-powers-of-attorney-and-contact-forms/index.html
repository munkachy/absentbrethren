Updating Wills, Powers of Attorney, and Contact Forms

Thursday, October 8, 2026

A memo from Fr. Bede, our Prior, dated October 7:

Over the coming weeks, the community will be updating three important documents for each monk — part of ordinary stewardship, to keep records current and reduce confusion when something serious happens. Instructions and a schedule will follow once Abbot Brendan returns from retreat; nothing is needed from anyone immediately.

A Last Will and Testament gives legally binding instructions for what must be settled after death, so a monk's personal circumstances and the monastery's interests are both addressed clearly under civil law.

A Power of Attorney lets someone act on a monk's behalf in legal, financial, and medical matters if he's ever unable to decide for himself. The revised version also builds in guidance from Catholic moral teaching — on the dignity of human life, proportionate treatment, ordinary care, nutrition and hydration, and pain management.

A Who to Contact form isn't a legal document — it just lists who the monastery should reach out to, with current contact information, in case of serious illness, hospitalization, emergency, or death.

Joanna will serve as Medical Witness where needed, and Br. Thomas as Notary Public.
