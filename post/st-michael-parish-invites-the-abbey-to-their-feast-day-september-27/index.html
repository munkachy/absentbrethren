St. Michael Parish Invites the Abbey to Their Feast Day — September 27

Thursday, September 10, 2026

Fr. Patrick Egan, O.S.B., Pastor of St. Michael Parish, wrote to invite Abbot Brendan and all the monks of the Abbey to their Saint Michael's Feast Day celebration on September 27, 2026.

Dinner will be served at 4 PM in the gymnasium, with Vespers to follow in the Church around 5–5:30 PM. The parish has asked to know how many monks plan to come so they can prepare properly — sign-up is requested by September 18 for anyone who'd like to attend.
