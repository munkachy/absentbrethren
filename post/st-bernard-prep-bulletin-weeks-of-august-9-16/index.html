St. Bernard Prep Bulletin — Weeks of August 9 & 16

Thursday, August 13, 2026

The Prep school's newsletter for the two weeks ahead — full details below.

Week of August 9–15

  • Sunday, Aug 9 — Mass 10:30 AM; Orientation; Lunch 11:45 AM; Parent/Student Information Session 12:45 PM, Swisher Building Room 100
  • Monday, Aug 10 (St. Lawrence) — Classes begin; Mass with transfer of the Holy Eucharist, 10:40 AM; welcome back!
  • Tuesday, Aug 11 (St. Clare) — Class pictures, 1st and 2nd periods, for 7th, 8th, and 9th grades
  • Wednesday, Aug 12 — Class pictures, 1st and 2nd periods, for 10th, 11th, and 12th grades
  • Thursday, Aug 13 — HS Dance, 7–9 PM, in the Byre
  • Friday, Aug 14 (St. Maximilian Kolbe) — Blue and White Day; lunch provided free for all students; 2 PM dismissal
  • Saturday, Aug 15 (The Assumption of the Virgin Mary)

Week of August 16–22

  • Wednesday, Aug 19 — Class meetings with elections
  • Thursday, Aug 20 (Solemnity of St. Bernard) — Mass, 11 AM
  • Friday, Aug 21 (The Queenship of the Blessed Virgin Mary)

Announcements

  • Blue and White Day (Friday) splits the high school into two teams competing in events throughout the day; lunch is free for everyone, and school dismisses at 2 PM.
  • The HS back-to-school dance is Thursday night in the Byre.
  • Scholars' Bowl, an academic competition running all year for both middle school and high school teams, is open to any student, new or returning — see Br. Thomas in the library or Dr. Mann in his classroom to join.

Happy birthday to everyone on campus who celebrated a summer birthday!
