The Story Behind Abbot Brendan's November 21 Blessing

Tuesday, August 18, 2026

We've already shared that Abbot Brendan's Abbatial Blessing is set for Saturday, November 21 — here's a bit more on how that date came to be.

Ordinarily, a newly elected Abbot receives the Abbatial Blessing within three months of election. But since Abbot Brendan is still working through diaconate ordination first, the Congregation's Abbot President, Abbot Jonathan R. Licari, OSB, consulted the Presidents Council and formally dispensed with that three-month rule so the Blessing could follow his diaconate ordination instead.

Abbot Licari's letter closed with every good wish "as you work toward diaconate ordination and Abbatial Blessing" — worth keeping both in prayer as that day approaches.
