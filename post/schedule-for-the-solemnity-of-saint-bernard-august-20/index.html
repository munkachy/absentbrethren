Schedule for the Solemnity of Saint Bernard — August 20

Tuesday, August 11, 2026

  • 10:30 A.M. — Sext
  • 11:00 A.M. — Mass (with SBP)
  • 5:30 P.M. — Vespers
  • 6:00 P.M. — Supper
  • 7:15 P.M. — Compline
