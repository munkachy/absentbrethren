Notes from the Community Board — August 24, 2026

Monday, August 24, 2026

Sacred Heart Monastery is giving away books

Sr. Lynn Elisabeth Meadows, OSB wrote to Prior Dominic this week on behalf of Sacred Heart Monastery: they're significantly downsizing their library and have invited any of us to come select books before they're gone. The window is short — today through Saturday, August 29, during the day — arrange a time with Sr. Marian at (256) 347-8650. Worth a prayer for the Sisters at Sacred Heart, and maybe a few new volumes for someone's cell.

Chapter meets Wednesday for the Election of Seniors

The community gathers in Chapter this Wednesday, August 26, at 3:30 PM, for the Election of Seniors — worth holding in prayer as the brothers who'll serve in that council to the Abbot are chosen.
