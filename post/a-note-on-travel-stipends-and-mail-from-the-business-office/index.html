A Note on Travel Stipends and Mail from the Business Office

Thursday, August 27, 2026

A memo from the Abbey Business Office, dated August 26:

Vacation and travel stipends will be ready for pickup — or left in mailboxes — the day before the departure date listed on the travel form (which needs to be submitted in advance). If departure falls on a Sunday or Monday, stipends go out the Friday before instead. Anyone needing a stipend earlier should talk to Zoie ahead of time.

The memo also points back to Abbot Brendan's mail policy, and asks that any bills or insurance correspondence meant for the business office be opened first, with charges initialed if they look correct.

Signed by Br. Charles, Zoie, and Sherri.
