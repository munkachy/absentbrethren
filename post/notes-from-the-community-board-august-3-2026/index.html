Notes from the Community Board — August 3, 2026

Tuesday, August 4, 2026

Prayer request: Michael Albright

A note on the board asks for prayer for Michael Albright, a St. Bernard Prep graduate. A knee replacement surgery earlier this year hasn't gone well, and he remains unable to walk.

A shared meal that means more this year

On Thursday, faculty join the monks for Sext and lunch in the refectory — observed formally, with prayers and a reading from the Holy Rule, just as after Abbot Brendan's election. Fr. Joel wrote that it means a great deal to him that the faculty treasure the Abbey's history and the monks of today.

Liturgical norms set for Abbot Brendan

The Master of Ceremonies has issued formal norms for how Mass and the Divine Office are celebrated with the new Abbot — one more sign of the community settling into this chapter.

Dominican Sisters' vows — one made, one ahead

Four Sisters of the Dominican Sisters of St. Cecilia Congregation (Nashville) made perpetual profession on July 25: Sr. Caterina Joy Tatoli, Sr. Evelyn Rose Morse, Sr. Maria Trinity Dagher, and Sr. Agnes Rose MacKellar. Seven more make first profession on August 10. Sr. Jane Dominic, leading this week's retreat, is from this same community.
