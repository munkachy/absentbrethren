Faculty Return for In-Service Days

Tuesday, August 4, 2026

Faculty and staff return August 5–7 for this year's In-Service days. This year's formation theme is "Encountering Suffering in Our Own Lives and the Lives of Those We Serve," opened by Fr. Joel and carried further by Sr. Jane Dominic, O.P. — a good one to hold in prayer, both for the faculty sitting with it and for whatever it is meant to address.

The days include Mass in the Abbey Church, and on Thursday the faculty join the monks for Midday Prayer (Sext) and lunch in the monastic refectory. Fr. Paschal and Fr. Joel are giving the year's President's and Headmaster's reports, and Rachael and Fr. Dominic are leading a session on the school's vision. The rest of the week is routine training and classroom preparation.
