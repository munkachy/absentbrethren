Visitors This Week, and a Leadership Swap

Saturday, October 3, 2026

A couple of updates from the board:

The community is welcoming two visitors this week: Brother Moses Owen, a Dominican Friar, arriving Sunday evening for a stay from October 4–10; and Father Andy Forsyth, arriving Monday mid-morning for October 5–7.

And effective this evening at First Vespers of Sunday, Fr. Bede and Fr. Dominic are swapping roles: Fr. Bede now serves as Prior, and Fr. Dominic as Subprior.
