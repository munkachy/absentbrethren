Upcoming Liturgical Events — Abbot Brendan's Blessing and Diaconate Ordination

Monday, November 9, 2026

A notice from Abbot Brendan, posted November 9, giving the schedule for three big days ahead — worth holding all of it in prayer.

Friday, November 20

Solemn Vespers, with Abbot President Jonathan Licari presiding, at 5:30 PM, followed by dinner in the Byre.

Saturday, November 21

Abbatial Blessing at 10:30 AM, followed by a festive lunch with guests.

Saturday, November 28

Diaconate Ordination at 10:30 AM, followed by an informal lunch with guests.

Worth noting for anyone keeping track: the diaconate ordination had earlier been set for October 20 and has since moved to November 28, now following the Abbatial Blessing rather than preceding it.
