Notes from the Community Board — October 19, 2026

Tuesday, October 20, 2026

Please pray for Bill in the kitchen

Br. Thomas asks prayers for Bill, who works in the kitchen. According to Caroline Rouhan, he is at UAB and may have congestive heart failure.

Please pray for the new girls' basketball coach

Br. Thomas also asks prayers for the Prep's new girls' basketball coach, who is in remission from a disease that attacks the B cells in her body.

Welcome, Fr. Luiz Alvarez

Br. Christopher asks the community to welcome Fr. Luiz Alvarez of the Archdiocese of Atlanta, who is staying with us October 19–23.

Painting this week, October 19–23

A notice from the Prior: the painters are working on the carport and the walkway leading to the monastery this week.

  • Cars may need to be parked outside the carport for now. Watch for areas that have been blocked off, and feel free to park near the back door when there's room.
  • Parking spaces will be blocked off as the work moves along, to keep the inconvenience small.
  • The old garage will not be painted this time; it will be part of a future project.

Thanks to everyone for their patience while the cleaning and painting are finished.
